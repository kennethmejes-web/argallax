ARGALLAX · satellite hotspot calendar · Philippines, 2002–2026

Did the burning change, or did the satellite change?

NASA has watched fires from space with two generations of instruments. The newer one (VIIRS, from 2012) records hotspots on about six times as many place-days as the older one (MODIS). ARGALLAX puts the whole record on one tested scale, so a change in the satellite is not mistaken for a change on the ground.
