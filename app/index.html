Did the burning change, or did the satellite change?

Satellite hotspot detections around the Philippines, 2002–2026. Three satellites recorded them in turn, and a change of satellite can look like a change in fire.

  1. Aqua MODISAug 2002 – Jan 20121 km sensor. Converted to the S-NPP scale on request.
  2. S-NPP VIIRSFeb 2012 – Jul 2026375 m sensor. The reference scale.
  3. NOAA-20 VIIRSFrom Aug 2026Continues S-NPP. Recent months are provisional.

Shaded years: after mid-2021 the Aqua-to-S-NPP relation was noisier; the cause cannot be separated between Aqua's orbit drift, S-NPP ageing and real change.

85% → 16%

Tested before it was trusted. On 18 months not used for fitting or method selection (Jan 2020 – Jun 2021), converting Aqua to the S-NPP scale cut the difference from S-NPP in regional monthly totals from 85% to 16%. The selection rule was changed after the selection scores were seen; under the original rule, no correction would have been chosen. Agreement is with S-NPP detections, not with actual fire.
